# Part 1 — SEO Blog Posts

## 1A — Full SEO Blog Post

```
You are an expert SEO content writer. Write a high-quality, engaging, and SEO-optimized blog post using the details below.

BUSINESS INFO:
- Business name: [BUSINESS NAME]
- Industry/niche: [NICHE]
- Target audience: [AUDIENCE DESCRIPTION]
- Brand tone: [TONE — e.g., friendly and conversational]

BLOG POST DETAILS:
- Topic/Title: [BLOG POST TITLE]
- Primary keyword: [PRIMARY KEYWORD]
- Secondary keywords: [SECONDARY KEYWORDS]
- Word count: [700 / 1200 / 1800] words
- Goal of this post: [GOAL — e.g., rank on Google for X, promote product Y]
- Call to action at the end: [CTA — e.g., "Download our free guide", "Book a free call"]

MUST INCLUDE:
- [ANY SPECIFIC POINTS OR SECTIONS THE CLIENT REQUESTED]

MUST AVOID:
- [ANY TOPICS OR WORDS TO AVOID]

FORMAT:
- Use H2 and H3 subheadings to break up the content
- Include a compelling intro that hooks the reader in the first 2 sentences
- Use short paragraphs (2–3 sentences max)
- Include a meta description at the end (150–160 characters) using the primary keyword
- Write naturally — do not keyword stuff

Write the full blog post now.
```

## 1B — Blog Post Outline

```
You are an SEO content strategist. Create a detailed blog post outline for the following:

Topic: [BLOG POST TITLE]
Primary keyword: [PRIMARY KEYWORD]
Secondary keywords: [SECONDARY KEYWORDS]
Target audience: [AUDIENCE]
Word count goal: [WORD COUNT]
Goal: [GOAL OF THE POST]

Create an outline with:
- A compelling SEO title (include the keyword)
- An intro hook summary
- 5–7 H2 sections with 2–3 H3 subsections each
- Notes on what each section should cover
- A conclusion and CTA

After I approve the outline, I'll ask you to write each section.
```

# Part 2 — Email Sequences

## 2A — Welcome Sequence (3 Emails)

```
You are an expert email copywriter. Write a 3-part welcome email sequence for a new subscriber.

BUSINESS INFO:
- Business name: [BUSINESS NAME]
- What they sell: [PRODUCT/SERVICE]
- Target customer: [IDEAL CUSTOMER DESCRIPTION]
- Brand tone: [TONE]

SEQUENCE GOAL:
[e.g., Build trust, introduce the brand, and lead them toward booking a free call]

EMAIL DETAILS:
- Email 1 (send immediately): Welcome + deliver the lead magnet/freebie + set expectations
- Email 2 (send day 2): Share a valuable tip or story that builds credibility
- Email 3 (send day 4): Soft pitch — introduce the main product/service with a CTA

PRODUCT/SERVICE DETAILS:
- Name: [PRODUCT NAME]
- Price: [PRICE]
- Top 3 benefits: [BENEFITS]
- CTA link text: [e.g., "Book your free call", "Shop now", "Download here"]

TONE NOTES:
- [ANY SPECIFIC TONE INSTRUCTIONS]
- Avoid: [THINGS TO AVOID]

For each email write:
- Subject line (+ 1 alternative)
- Preview text (40–90 characters)
- Full email body

Write all 3 emails now.
```

## 2B — Sales Launch Sequence (5–7 Emails)

```
You are a conversion-focused email copywriter. Write a [5 or 7]-email sales sequence to launch/promote the product below.

BUSINESS INFO:
- Business: [BUSINESS NAME]
- Niche: [NICHE]
- Audience: [IDEAL CUSTOMER]
- Tone: [TONE]

PRODUCT DETAILS:
- Product name: [NAME]
- Price: [PRICE]
- What it does: [1–2 SENTENCE DESCRIPTION]
- Top benefits:
  1. [BENEFIT 1]
  2. [BENEFIT 2]
  3. [BENEFIT 3]
- Pain point it solves: [PAIN POINT]
- Testimonial/social proof: [PASTE TESTIMONIAL OR WRITE "none"]
- Urgency/deadline: [e.g., "Sale ends Friday", "Only 10 spots", or "none"]
- CTA: [e.g., "Buy now at 40% off", "Join the waitlist", "Book a call"]

EMAIL SEQUENCE STRUCTURE:
- Email 1 (Day 1): The problem — agitate the pain point
- Email 2 (Day 2): The solution — introduce the product
- Email 3 (Day 3): Social proof / case study
- Email 4 (Day 4): Handle objections (why people hesitate)
- Email 5 (Day 5): Urgency — deadline reminder
- Email 6 (Day 6): Last chance warning
- Email 7 (Day 7): Final hours / cart closing

For each email write:
- Subject line (+ 1 alternative)
- Preview text
- Full email body with a clear CTA

Write all emails now.
```

## 2D — Re-Engagement Email

```
Write a re-engagement email for subscribers who haven't opened emails in 60+ days.

Business: [BUSINESS NAME]
What they sell: [PRODUCT/SERVICE]
Tone: [TONE]
Incentive to come back (if any): [e.g., "10% off coupon", "free resource", or "none"]

The email should:
- Acknowledge they've been quiet (in a fun, non-guilt-tripping way)
- Remind them of the value of being on the list
- Give them a reason to re-engage
- Include a clear CTA

Write 3 subject line options and the full email.
```

# Part 3 — Bonus Utilities

## 3A — Upwork Proposal

```
Write a short, personalized Upwork proposal for the following job posting.

Job posting: [PASTE THE JOB DESCRIPTION HERE]

My background: I'm a content writer specializing in SEO blog posts and email sequences.
My tone should be: confident, friendly, and results-focused.

The proposal should:
- Open with a line that shows I actually read their posting (not generic)
- Briefly explain how I can solve their specific problem
- Mention 1 relevant result or example
- End with a clear, low-friction CTA (e.g., "Want to see a sample? Just ask.")
- Be under 150 words

Write the proposal now.
```

## 3C — Content Ideas

```
Generate 10 SEO blog post ideas for the following business:

Business: [BUSINESS NAME]
Niche: [NICHE]
Target audience: [AUDIENCE]
Main product/service: [PRODUCT OR SERVICE]
Goal: [e.g., drive organic traffic, establish authority, promote products]

For each idea include:
- Blog post title (SEO-friendly, includes a keyword)
- Target keyword
- One sentence on what the post covers
- Estimated search intent (informational / commercial / navigational)

Format as a numbered list.
```

---

## Golden Rules
1. Fill in **every** bracket — vague input = vague output
2. Read the output before sending — never deliver raw AI output
3. Change at least 10–15% of the content (add a personal line, fix awkward phrases)
4. Save prompts that worked great — note which ones got the best results
5. If output is weak, add more client detail and run it again
