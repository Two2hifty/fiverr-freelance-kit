# CLIENT COMMUNICATION TEMPLATES
### Every message you'll ever need — copy, personalize, send.

---

## WHY THIS MATTERS

Your writing skill gets the order. Your communication skill keeps the client.
Most freelancers lose repeat business not because their work was bad — but because they went silent, sounded defensive, or handled a difficult moment poorly.

These templates cover every stage of the client relationship, from first inquiry to long-term retainer.

---

## SECTION 1: PRE-ORDER MESSAGES

### 1A — Responding to a Buyer Inquiry (Fast + Professional)

**Use when:** A buyer messages asking about your services before ordering.

```
Hi [Name]! Thanks for reaching out.

I'd love to help with [what they described]. To make sure I can deliver exactly what you need, a few quick questions:

1. What's the topic/niche?
2. Who's your target audience?
3. Do you have a target keyword (for blog posts) or a main product/goal (for email sequences)?

Once I know a bit more, I can confirm the right package and timeline for you.

Looking forward to it!
[Your name]
```

---

### 1B — Responding When They Want Something Outside Your Packages

**Use when:** A buyer asks for something you don't offer as standard.

```
Hi [Name]! Great question.

That's a bit outside my standard packages, but I can absolutely put together a custom offer for you.

To give you an accurate quote, could you share:
- How many pieces you need
- Approximate word count / length
- Your timeline

I'll come back to you with a custom package within a few hours.
```

---

### 1C — Sending a Custom Offer

**Use when:** Following up after scoping a custom request.

```
Hi [Name]! Based on what you shared, here's a custom offer:

WHAT'S INCLUDED:
- [List deliverables clearly]
- [Revisions included]
- Delivery by: [date]

PRICE: $[amount]

This covers everything we discussed. If you'd like to adjust anything before accepting, just let me know.

Ready when you are!
```

## SECTION 2: DURING THE ORDER

### 2A — Order Confirmation + Intake Reminder

**Use when:** Someone places an order without filling out the intake form.

```
Hi [Name]! Order received — thank you!

To get started right away, I just need a few details from you. Please fill out this quick form: [link to Google Form / Notion page]

Or if you'd prefer, just reply with:
- Topic / keyword target
- Target audience
- Your brand tone (e.g., professional, friendly, bold)
- Any specific points to include

The more detail you give me, the better your content will be. I'll start as soon as I hear back!
```

---

### 2B — Proactive Progress Update

**Use when:** Order is in progress and you want to build confidence (especially for 3+ day orders).

```
Hi [Name]! Quick update — I'm deep into your [blog post / email sequence] and it's coming together well.

I'm on track to deliver [on time / a day early]. If anything has changed on your end or you want to add any details, now's a great time to let me know.

Otherwise, see you at delivery!
```

---

### 2C — Requesting a Deadline Extension

**Use when:** You genuinely need more time (rare, but handle it proactively).

```
Hi [Name], I want to be upfront with you.

To deliver the quality you're expecting, I'd like to request a short extension — [1 / 2] extra day(s). I could deliver on time, but I'd rather take the extra time than rush and shortchange you.

Would that work for you? I'll make sure it's worth the wait.
```

## SECTION 3: DELIVERY MESSAGES

### 3A — Standard Delivery Message

**Use when:** Delivering any completed order.

```
Hi [Name]! Your [blog post / email sequence] is ready — attached below.

A few notes on what I've done:
- [Specific choice 1 — e.g., "I used a story-based intro to hook readers immediately"]
- [Specific choice 2 — e.g., "The keyword appears in the title, intro, 2 H2s, and conclusion"]
- [Specific choice 3 — e.g., "Email 3 uses a soft close rather than a hard pitch to match your brand tone"]

If anything needs adjusting, just ask — revisions are included.

If you're happy with the work, a review would mean a lot. Thanks for trusting me with your content!
[Your name]
```

---

### 3B — Delivery with Proactive Upsell

**Use when:** Delivering work and the natural next step is obvious.

```
Hi [Name]! Delivery attached — hope you love it.

[Brief 1-sentence note on a specific quality decision you made]

One thing I noticed while writing: [observation about their content needs — e.g., "your audience would probably respond really well to a 3-email welcome sequence to go with this post"]. Happy to put something together if you'd like.

As always, revisions are open — just let me know!
```

## SECTION 4: REVISION HANDLING

### 4A — Acknowledging a Revision Request Professionally

**Use when:** Client requests a revision.

```
Hi [Name]! No problem at all — that's what revisions are for.

I've read through your feedback. Just to make sure I nail it this time:

[Restate their feedback in your own words to confirm you understood]

Is that right? I'll get the revision back to you within [timeframe].
```

---

### 4B — Handling Vague Revision Feedback

**Use when:** Client says something like "it's not quite right" without specifics.

```
Hi [Name]! Happy to revise — I want to make sure I get it right for you.

Could you help me understand what specifically isn't hitting the mark? For example:

- Is it the tone? (Too formal / too casual?)
- The structure? (Too long / missing a section?)
- The angle? (Not speaking to the right audience?)

Even a sentence or two of direction helps a lot. Once I know, I'll turn it around quickly.
```

---

### 4C — Handling Scope Creep Gracefully

**Use when:** A client asks for revisions that go beyond what was agreed.

```
Hi [Name]! Happy to help with this.

Just a heads up — what you're describing goes a bit beyond the original scope (you've asked for [X], and the order covered [Y]).

I can absolutely do this — I'd just need to put together a small add-on offer to cover the extra work. Does that sound fair?

Let me know and I'll get it sorted straight away.
```

## SECTION 5: DIFFICULT SITUATIONS

### 5A — Responding to a Negative or Unfair Review

**Use when:** A client leaves a review that doesn't reflect the situation accurately.

```
Hi [Name], thank you for the feedback — I'm sorry the work didn't meet your expectations.

I'd genuinely like to make this right. Could you share specifically what didn't hit the mark? I'm happy to revise, rewrite, or explore solutions.

My goal is always 100% satisfaction. Let me know how I can fix this for you.
[Your name]
```

*Note: Keep this short, calm, and solution-focused. Fiverr allows sellers to respond publicly to reviews — if you do, potential buyers will read your response. Professionalism here is marketing.*

---

### 5B — Client Goes Silent After Order Placement

**Use when:** You have an active order but the client hasn't responded to your intake questions.

```
Hi [Name]! Just checking in — I have your order ready to start but I'm still waiting on a few details from the intake form.

[Resend the 3–4 most critical questions inline]

If I don't hear back in 24 hours, I'll proceed with my best judgment based on your order brief — but more detail always leads to better output.

Happy to start whenever you're ready!
```

---

### 5C — Requesting a Mutual Cancellation (Last Resort)

**Use when:** A project has become genuinely unworkable and you need to exit cleanly.

```
Hi [Name], I want to be straightforward with you.

After [brief factual description of situation], I don't think I'm able to deliver what you're looking for to the standard you deserve.

Rather than deliver something that won't meet your needs, I'd like to suggest a mutual cancellation so you can find a better fit. There's no hard feelings on my end — I just want to be honest with you.

Let me know your thoughts.
```

*Note: Mutual cancellations still affect your metrics. Use sparingly and only after exhausting every other option.*

## SECTION 6: RETENTION & REPEAT BUSINESS

### 6A — Following Up After Delivery (3–5 Days Later)

**Use when:** Order is marked complete. No review yet, or you want to re-engage for repeat business.

```
Hi [Name]! Just wanted to check in — how has the [blog post / email sequence] been performing?

If you're planning more content, I'd love to keep working together. I already know your brand voice, which means faster turnaround and better results each time.

Let me know if there's anything in the pipeline!
```

---

### 6B — Pitching a Monthly Retainer

**Use when:** A client has ordered 2+ times and is a reliable source of repeat work.

```
Hi [Name]! It's been great working on your content.

I wanted to mention — I offer a monthly retainer for clients who need consistent output:

OPTION A: 4 blog posts/month — $[X] (save [Y]% vs. individual orders)
OPTION B: 2 blog posts + 1 email sequence/month — $[X]
OPTION C: Custom — tell me what you need monthly and I'll build a package

Benefits for you:
- Priority delivery (you skip the queue)
- Consistent brand voice across all content
- Flat monthly rate — no surprises

Would you like me to put together a custom retainer proposal?
```

---

### 6C — Re-engaging a Past Client Who Has Gone Quiet

**Use when:** A client you worked with hasn't returned in 60+ days.

```
Hi [Name]! Hope things are going well with [their business / niche if you remember].

I was thinking about the [blog post / email sequence] I wrote for you a while back and wondered how it's been performing.

If you have more content coming up, I'd love to pick up where we left off — I still have your brand voice notes on file.

No pressure at all — just wanted to stay in touch!
```

## QUICK REFERENCE: TONE CALIBRATION

| Situation | Tone to Use |
|---|---|
| First inquiry response | Warm, fast, professional |
| During order | Proactive, confident, brief |
| Delivery | Specific, appreciative, forward-looking |
| Revision request | Calm, solution-focused, no defensiveness |
| Difficult client | Neutral, factual, professional |
| Retention pitch | Friendly, value-focused, low pressure |

## GOLDEN RULES FOR CLIENT COMMUNICATION

1. **Respond within 1 hour** during your working hours — response time is a ranking factor on Fiverr
2. **Never go silent** — if you need time, send a quick acknowledgement
3. **Restate before you revise** — confirm you understood the feedback before doing work
4. **Never be defensive** — even when you're right
5. **Every message is a chance to build trust** — or erode it
6. **Keep messages scannable** — short paragraphs, bullets for lists, clear ask at the end
7. **Name the client** — start messages with their name; it signals you see them as a person, not a ticket
