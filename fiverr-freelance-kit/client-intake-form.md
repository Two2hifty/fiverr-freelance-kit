# CLIENT INTAKE FORM
### Send this to every client after they place an order

---

## HOW TO USE THIS
Copy the questions below and paste them into:
- A Google Form (free, looks professional)
- A Notion page
- Or just send as a Fiverr/Upwork message

---

## INTAKE FORM — SEO BLOG POST

```
Hi [Client Name]! Thanks for your order.
To write content that perfectly fits your brand, please answer the questions below.
The more detail you give, the better your content will be!

---

ABOUT YOUR BUSINESS
1. What is your business/website name?
2. What do you sell or offer?
3. Who is your target audience? (age, gender, interests, pain points)
4. What is your website URL? (if you have one)

ABOUT THIS BLOG POST
5. What is the main topic or title of the blog post?
6. What is your target keyword? (the phrase you want to rank for on Google)
7. Are there any secondary keywords to include?
8. What is the main goal of this article?
   [ ] Drive traffic from Google
   [ ] Educate my audience
   [ ] Promote a product/service
   [ ] Build authority in my niche
   [ ] Other: ___________

9. What should the reader DO after reading? (e.g., buy a product, sign up, book a call)
10. Are there any specific points, sections, or facts you want included?
11. Are there any competitors or articles you like the style of? (share URLs)

BRAND VOICE
12. How would you describe your brand tone?
   [ ] Professional and formal
   [ ] Friendly and conversational
   [ ] Witty and fun
   [ ] Bold and direct
   [ ] Educational and informative

13. Any words, phrases, or topics to AVOID?
14. Any specific words or phrases you always use?

DELIVERY PREFERENCES
15. Do you want the article formatted with H2/H3 headings? [ ] Yes [ ] No
16. Do you want a meta description included? [ ] Yes [ ] No
17. Any other notes or special requests?
```

## INTAKE FORM — EMAIL SEQUENCE

```
Hi [Client Name]! Thanks for your order.
Please fill out the details below so I can write emails that speak directly to your audience.

---

ABOUT YOUR BUSINESS
1. What is your business name?
2. What product or service are these emails promoting?
3. Who are your subscribers? (describe your ideal customer)
4. What is the price of the product/service being promoted?

ABOUT THE EMAIL SEQUENCE
5. What type of sequence do you need?
   [ ] Welcome sequence (new subscribers)
   [ ] Sales/launch sequence (selling a product)
   [ ] Nurture sequence (building trust over time)
   [ ] Abandoned cart sequence
   [ ] Re-engagement sequence (win back cold subscribers)
   [ ] Other: ___________

6. How many emails do you want in the sequence?
7. How many days apart should each email be sent?
8. What is the ONE main goal of this sequence? (e.g., get people to buy X, book a call, download Y)

THE OFFER
9. What problem does your product/service solve?
10. What are the top 3 benefits of your product/service?
    - Benefit 1:
    - Benefit 2:
    - Benefit 3:
11. Do you have any testimonials or success stories I can reference? (paste them here)
12. Is there a discount, bonus, or deadline to create urgency?

BRAND VOICE
13. How would you describe your email tone?
    [ ] Professional
    [ ] Conversational / friendly
    [ ] Story-driven
    [ ] Straight to the point
    [ ] Humorous

14. Share 1–2 emails you've sent before (or a competitor's emails you like)
15. Anything to AVOID in the emails?

DELIVERY
16. What email platform do you use? (Mailchimp, Klaviyo, ConvertKit, etc.)
17. Any formatting preferences?
18. Any other notes?
```

---

## TIPS FOR USING THE INTAKE FORM

- Always send this BEFORE starting any work
- If a client skips a question, message them and ask — better info = better output
- Save all responses in a folder organized by client name
- Use their exact words in your prompts (especially their tone descriptions)
- The intake form also makes you look professional and organized
