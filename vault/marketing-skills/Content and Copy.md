# Content and Copy Skills

Seven skills cover writing, editing, and content planning.

---

## Copywriting `/copywriting`

Write or rewrite marketing pages: homepage, landing pages, pricing, feature pages, about pages.

### Core principles
- Clarity beats cleverness. If you must choose, choose clear.
- Benefits over features. Features = what it does. Benefits = what that means for the customer.
- Specificity over vagueness. "Cut reporting from 4 hours to 15 minutes" beats "save time."
- Customer language over company language. Mirror words from reviews and support tickets.
- One idea per section. Build a logical argument down the page.

### What it asks
- Page type and primary conversion goal
- Target audience, their problem, and their objections
- What makes the product different
- Traffic source context (what visitors already know)

### Strong headline patterns
- Outcome-focused: "Get [outcome] without [pain point]"
- Specific: Include numbers, timeframes, concrete details
- Social proof: "Join 10,000+ teams who..."

## Copy Editing `/copy-editing`

Improve existing copy through seven sequential passes.

### The Seven Sweeps Framework
Each pass focuses on one dimension only:
1. Clarity sweep — cut confusion
2. Brevity sweep — cut words
3. Specificity sweep — replace vague with concrete
4. Voice sweep — match brand tone
5. Benefit sweep — flip features to outcomes
6. CTA sweep — sharpen calls to action
7. Final read — catch anything missed

### Key principle
Do not rewrite. Enhance. Each edit needs a clear reason.

## Email Sequence `/email-sequence`

Design automated email flows for any lifecycle stage.

### Sequence types
- Welcome/onboarding
- Lead nurture
- Re-engagement
- Post-purchase
- Sales/launch
- Educational

### Core principles
1. One email, one job. One primary CTA per email.
2. Value before ask. Build trust through content before selling.
3. Relevance over volume. Fewer, better emails win.
4. Clear path forward. Every email moves the subscriber somewhere.

### What it asks
- Sequence type and trigger
- Who the audience is and what they already know
- Primary conversion goal
- Number of emails and send cadence

## Cold Email `/cold-email`

Write B2B outreach emails and follow-up sequences that get replies.

### Before writing, gather
1. Who you're writing to (role, company, why them)
2. What you want (meeting, reply, demo)
3. What value you deliver for people like them
4. Proof: result, case study, credibility signal
5. Research signals: funding, hiring, news, tech stack

### Writing principles
- Sound like a sharp human, not a sales machine
- Personalize to the specific person or signal
- Keep it short. Shorter than you think.
- One ask per email
- Low-friction CTA (15 minutes, not "jump on a call")

## Social Content `/social-content`

Create and repurpose social media content for any platform.

### Platform quick reference
| Platform | Best for | Frequency | Key format |
|---|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn | B2B, thought leadership | 3 to 5x/week | Carousels, text posts |
| Twitter/X | Tech, real-time, community | 3 to 10x/day | Threads, hot takes |
| Instagram | Visual brands, lifestyle | 1 to 2 posts + Stories daily | Reels, carousels |
| TikTok | Brand awareness, younger audience | 1 to 4x/day | Short-form video |
| Facebook | Communities, local businesses | 1 to 2x/day | Groups, native video |

### Content pillars framework
Build around 3 to 5 pillars that align with your expertise and your audience's interests. Mix:
- Educational (how-to, tips)
- Social proof (results, testimonials)
- Behind-the-scenes (process, team)
- Direct offer (CTA posts)
- Engagement (questions, opinions)

## Content Strategy `/content-strategy`

Plan what content to produce and why.

### Two content types
1. **Searchable**: Targets keywords. Drives organic traffic. Compounds over time.
2. **Shareable**: Gets distributed by audience. Builds brand and authority.

### What it asks
- Business goals for content (traffic, leads, thought leadership)
- Questions customers ask before buying
- Objections that come up in sales calls
- Topics that appear in support tickets
- Language customers use to describe their problems

### Output
- Content pillars
- Topic clusters
- Editorial calendar
- Content briefs for individual pieces

## Lead Magnets `/lead-magnets`

Plan and design email capture offers.

### Lead magnet types
- Checklists and cheat sheets (fast to create, high perceived value)
- Templates (Notion, spreadsheet, Google Doc)
- Short guides or ebooks
- Email courses
- Free tools or calculators (see `/free-tool-strategy`)
- Resource libraries
- Webinars and workshops

### What makes a strong lead magnet
- Solves one specific problem
- Delivers value in under 10 minutes
- Naturally leads toward your product
- Targets the right stage of awareness
