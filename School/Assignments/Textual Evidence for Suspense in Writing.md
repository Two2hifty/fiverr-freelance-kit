## Tips
- Descriptive writing: look for long, vivid passages that slow the reader down and build dread
- Setting: find where the location itself feels threatening or unfamiliar
- Sensory details: smell, sound, texture — things that make a scene feel real and unsettling
- Pacing/delay: short sentences that stretch a moment out, or paragraphs that withhold information
