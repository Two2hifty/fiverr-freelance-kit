## Main Goal

**$1,000 in the first month**

## Revenue Breakdown (how to hit $1k)

| Scenario | Orders Needed | Price | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| All Basic ($15 launch price) | ~67 orders | $15 | $1,005 |
| All Standard ($45 launch price) | ~23 orders | $45 | $1,035 |
| Mix: 10 Standard + 5 Premium | 15 orders | $45/$99 | $945 |
| **Realistic mix** | **~20 orders** | **avg $50** | **$1,000** |

> [!tip] Aim for Standard package orders — best value for the effort

## Phase 1: Launch (Month 1)

- [ ] Publish gig with launch pricing (Basic $15 / Standard $45 / Premium $99)
- [ ] Send 10 buyer requests per day
- [ ] Deliver every order in under 48 hours
- [ ] Ask every satisfied client for a review
- [ ] Goal: get 10 five-star reviews ASAP

## Phase 2: Scale (After 10 Reviews)

- [ ] Raise prices to full rate (Basic $25 / Standard $75 / Premium $150)
- [ ] Add a second gig or upsell (e.g., content calendar, social captions)
- [ ] Build repeat client relationships
