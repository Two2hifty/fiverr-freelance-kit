# Sales and GTM Skills

Two skills cover the systems and collateral that connect marketing to revenue.

---

## RevOps `/revops`

Design and optimize the systems connecting marketing, sales, and customer success.

### What it asks
- GTM motion: product-led (PLG), sales-led, or hybrid
- ACV range (average contract value)
- Sales cycle length (days from first touch to closed-won)
- Current tech stack: CRM, marketing automation, scheduling, enrichment tools
- Current lead management process
- Goals: increase conversion, reduce speed-to-lead, fix handoff leaks, or build from scratch

### Core areas

**Lead lifecycle design:**
- MQL definition: what signals qualify a marketing lead?
- SQL definition: what signals indicate sales-readiness?
- Lead scoring model: how do you score behavior and firmographics?
- Lead routing: who gets which leads and how fast?

**Speed-to-lead:**
- 78% of buyers go with the first vendor to respond
- Target: less than 5 minutes from form submit to first contact
- Use tools like Chili Piper or Calendly for instant scheduling

**Marketing-to-sales handoff:**
- What data passes from marketing to sales?
- What does a sales rep see in the CRM when a lead is assigned?
- Who owns leads that are not yet SQL?

**Pipeline management:**
- Stage definitions that align marketing and sales
- Exit criteria for each stage (not activity, but outcome)
- SLAs for follow-up at each stage

## Sales Enablement `/sales-enablement`

Create collateral that helps sales teams close deals.

### Asset types

**Pitch deck**: Used in sales calls and demos. Covers problem, solution, differentiators, proof, and next steps.

**One-pager / leave-behind**: One page (two sides max). Problem, solution, key benefits, social proof, pricing overview, CTA. Left with a prospect after a meeting.

**Objection handling doc**: A reference doc for common objections with approved responses. Built from real sales call recordings.

**Demo script / talk track**: A structured flow for product demos. Covers discovery questions, key moments to show, objections to handle, and closing language.

**Buyer persona card**: A one-page profile of each key buyer type. Role, goals, pain points, typical objections, what they care about most.

**ROI calculator**: A tool showing the financial return from buying. Input: current costs or metrics. Output: projected savings or gains.

### What it asks
- Value proposition and key differentiators
- GTM motion (self-serve, inside sales, field sales)
- Average deal size and sales cycle length
- Key personas involved in the buying decision
- Specific collateral needed

### What makes collateral actually used
- It must be short. Sales reps do not read long documents.
- It must be accurate. Wrong pricing or outdated features destroys trust.
- It must be specific. Generic decks do not help close specific deals.
- It must be findable. A Google Drive folder with 200 files is as bad as nothing.
