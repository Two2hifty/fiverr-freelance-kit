## Goal: $1,000 in Month 1

### What that actually looks like
| Package | Price | Orders needed to hit $1k |
|---|---|---|
| Basic | $15 | 67 orders |
| Standard | $45 | 23 orders |
| Premium | $99 | 11 orders |
| **Mixed (realistic)** | avg ~$45 | **~22–25 orders** |

> [!tip] Best path to $1k fast
> Focus on getting **Standard package** orders ($45). At 22–23 orders in a month that's ~1 order per day — very achievable once the gig has a few reviews.

## Month 1 Roadmap

### Week 1 — Launch
- [ ] Publish gig with launch pricing (Basic $15 / Standard $45 / Premium $99)
- [ ] Create 3 Canva gig images
- [ ] Share gig link on social media (TikTok, Instagram, Twitter/X)
- [ ] Send gig to 5 people who might need content writing
- [ ] Apply to 5–10 Fiverr Buyer Requests per day

### Week 2 — Get First Reviews
- [ ] Aim for 3–5 completed orders
- [ ] Deliver early, over-communicate, ask for reviews politely
- [ ] Refine intake form based on real client experience
- [ ] Respond to all messages within 1 hour (boosts Fiverr ranking)

### Week 3 — Build Momentum
- [ ] Aim for 10 five-star reviews
- [ ] Start raising prices once reviews are in
- [ ] Create a second gig (e.g., standalone email sequence)
- [ ] Track income in [[Income Tracker]]

### Week 4 — Scale
- [ ] Raise to full prices (Basic $25 / Standard $75 / Premium $150)
- [ ] Analyse which package is selling most
- [ ] Consider Fiverr Pro application if eligible
- [ ] Set Month 2 target

## Fiverr Ranking Tips
- Reply to every message within 1 hour
- Always deliver on time (never late)
- Keep order completion rate above 90%
- Ask happy clients for a review (do it naturally, not pushy)
- Stay online as much as possible in the first few weeks
