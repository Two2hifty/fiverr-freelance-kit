## PROMPT 1A — Full Social Media Growth Plan (Complete Package)

```
YOU ARE A WORLD-CLASS SOCIAL MEDIA STRATEGIST AND CONTENT CREATOR.
YOUR MISSION IS TO CREATE A COMPLETE SOCIAL MEDIA GROWTH PLAN FOR
THE BUSINESS BELOW, USING BOTH ORGANIC AND PAID STRATEGIES TO
MAXIMIZE REACH, ENGAGEMENT, AND CONVERSIONS.

---

BUSINESS INFO:
- Business name: [BUSINESS NAME]
- Business type / niche: [e.g., AI education, fitness coaching, eCommerce jewelry]
- Website URL: [WEBSITE URL or "none"]
- Main product or service: [PRODUCT/SERVICE]
- Price point: [PRICE OR RANGE]
- Current social media presence: [PLATFORMS + FOLLOWER COUNTS, or "starting from zero"]
- Monthly budget for paid ads (if any): [BUDGET or "organic only"]
- Top 3 goals: [e.g., grow followers, generate leads, drive sales]
- Brand tone: [e.g., professional, playful, bold, educational]
- Top 3 competitors: [COMPETITOR NAMES OR URLS, or "unknown"]

TARGET AUDIENCE:
- Age range: [AGE RANGE]
- Gender: [GENDER or "all"]
- Location: [LOCATION or "global"]
- Interests: [INTERESTS]
- Top pain points: [PAIN POINTS]
- Where they spend time online: [PLATFORMS/COMMUNITIES]

---

STEP 1 — BUSINESS & AUDIENCE DEEP DIVE:
- Identify 3–5 unique selling propositions (USPs) based on the business info above.
- Profile the target audience in detail: demographics, psychographics, interests, and
  top 3 pain points.
- Identify 3 content patterns or strategies that top competitors are using to win
  on social media.

STEP 2 — GROWTH STRATEGY:
- Recommend the top 2–4 social platforms to focus on and explain why each one
  is the right fit for this business and audience.
- Outline organic tactics: content pillars (3–5 themes), recommended posting
  frequency per platform, and engagement strategies (comments, DMs, collaborations).
- Outline paid tactics: best ad formats for each platform, targeting methods,
  3 specific creative ideas, and budget guidance.
- Provide 5 example messaging angles and hooks that align with the USPs and
  speak directly to the audience's pain points.

STEP 3 — VIRAL-INSPIRED 30-DAY CONTENT CALENDAR:
Create a fully detailed 30-day calendar as a table with these columns:
| Day | Platform | Content Type | Hook | Script Outline | Caption Copy |

Definitions:
- Platform: Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Facebook, X (Twitter)
- Content Type: Short-form video, Carousel, Static image, Story, Live, Poll,
  Blog repurpose, UGC-style, Behind-the-scenes, Testimonial, etc.
- Hook: The scroll-stopping first line or on-screen text (make it punchy)
- Script Outline: 4–6 bullet points covering the flow of the content
- Caption Copy: Full caption optimized for engagement and/or conversion,
  including relevant hashtags (5–10)

Distribute content across the recommended platforms. Mix educational, entertaining,
inspirational, and promotional content in a 70/20/10 ratio.

Deliver Step 1, Step 2, and Step 3 in full. Do not summarize — write everything out.
```

## PROMPT 1C — 30-Day Content Calendar Only

```
You are a social media content strategist. Create a detailed 30-day content
calendar for the business below.

BUSINESS INFO:
- Business: [BUSINESS NAME]
- Niche: [NICHE]
- Target audience: [AUDIENCE]
- Platforms to post on: [LIST PLATFORMS]
- Brand tone: [TONE]
- Main offer/product: [PRODUCT OR SERVICE]
- Content goal: [e.g., grow followers, drive traffic to website, generate leads]

Format the calendar as a table with these columns:
| Day | Platform | Content Type | Hook | Script Outline | Caption Copy |

Rules:
- Mix content types: short-form video, carousels, stories, static posts, polls
- Use a 70/20/10 content mix: 70% value/education, 20% entertainment/relatability,
  10% direct promotion
- Every hook must be scroll-stopping (question, bold statement, or surprising fact)
- Every caption must end with a call to action
- Include 5–10 relevant hashtags per post

Write all 30 days in full. Do not abbreviate.
```

## PROMPT 2A — Batch Caption Writer (10 Posts at Once)

```
Write 10 social media captions for the following business.

Niche: [NICHE]
Audience: [AUDIENCE]
Platform: [PLATFORM]
Tone: [TONE]

For each caption:
- Write a hook as the first line (make it punchy)
- Keep body copy concise and scannable
- End with a CTA (vary the CTAs — don't repeat the same one)
- Include 5–8 relevant hashtags

Mix the topics across: educational tips, behind-the-scenes, social proof,
product/service spotlight, and audience engagement (question or poll).

Label each caption 1–10 with a suggested content type (e.g., carousel, reel, story).

## PROMPT 2B — LinkedIn Thought Leadership Post

```
Write a high-performing LinkedIn post for the following professional/business.

Name/Brand: [NAME OR BUSINESS]
Industry: [INDUSTRY]
Topic: [TOPIC — e.g., a lesson learned, an industry insight, a personal story]

Write the post now.

16. How would you describe your brand tone?

PAID ADS (if applicable)

20. Any other notes, requests, or things I should know about your business?
